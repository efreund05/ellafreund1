About Ella Freund

Marketing-minded creativity rooted in clarity, warmth, and strong visuals.

Hi, I'm Ella

I am a senior marketing student at Iowa State University and a freelance designer-photographer who enjoys shaping visuals that connect with people. My background in marketing helps me think about more than aesthetics alone—I also care about message, audience, and brand consistency.

EF Creative is designed to be a flexible home for my portfolio, creative services, and future work. The layout is intentionally simple so I can easily refresh projects, swap imagery from Canva, and keep the site aligned with my evolving brand.

Mission statement

My mission is to create purposeful visuals and content that feel clean, personal, and strategic—helping brands and collaborators tell their story with confidence.
